# Error budget policy

Service / approved SLO / window:
Business owner / release owner / reliability owner:

| Condition | Action | Decision owner |
|---|---|---|
| Budget healthy and change risk acceptable | Normal release process | |
| Sustained rapid budget burn | Investigate and reduce change exposure | |
| Budget exhausted | Prioritize reliability work and review discretionary releases | |
| Security or urgent business exception | Record impact, mitigation and approval | |

Do not automatically halt every change. A fix that restores reliability may be the most important release. Agree exception authority and review dates in advance.
